I do like the sensitive side of this country! Today’s Media Guardian website reports that “Pot Noodle’s controversial adverts using the line The **slag of all snacks** have been banned from appearing on TV after more than 300 viewers complained”.

300 people actually bothered to complain about a television advertisement. I would really love to know why they felt this was inappropriate. I thought they were some of the funniest commercials that I have seen in a long time, and the word was used in a humorous, self-deprecating way. How can calling a plastic pot of fast food be that offensive? Such a shame they were pulled. However, I don’t really see the point, as most people have seen them anyway.

Anyway, here is a gratuitous link to the [most offensive words in the English language](http://media.guardian.co.uk/broadcast/table/0,7493,409833,00.html)! Wonder if any of the complainers have a website where they outline their complaints. I’d love to know. [[Ref: Media Guardian](http://media.guardian.co.uk/advertising/story/0,7492,775861,00.html)]
